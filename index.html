OUR HISTORY

THE STORY OF MABOPANE LFA

The Mabopane Local Football Association (LFA) was established with a vision to bring the community together through the beautiful game. What started as a small initiative has grown into a respected league, providing a platform for talent, unity and development in Mabopane and its surrounding areas.

Over the years, the LFA has played a vital role in nurturing young talent, encouraging fair play and creating opportunities for players, clubs and communities. Our mission remains the same — to grow football, uplift our people, and build a stronger Mabopane through sport.

MORE THAN A LEAGUE — A COMMUNITY

The Mabopane LFA is committed to developing football at the grassroots level, supporting our clubs and players, and creating a better future for the community.
