ABOUT US

The Mabopane Local Football Association (MABLFA) has been the heartbeat of our community since 1996.

Driven by a passion for the beautiful game, we create clear pathways for players, coaches, and referees to excel. We believe in the power of football to unite and inspire — cultivating talent on the field while building character off it.

From our youth academies to our senior leagues, MABLFA is dedicated to shaping the next generation of football stars and community leaders.

OUR MISSION

To develop football at the grassroots level, support our clubs and players, and create a better future for the Mabopane community through the power of sport.
