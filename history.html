HISTORY

ORGANIZATION OVERVIEW

Name of Organization: Mabopane Local Football Association

Bafana Bafana participated only for the second time ever in the FIFA World Cup in this year's iteration, the FIFA World Cup 2026. It is a disappointing fact considering the South African Premier Soccer League (PSL) is ranked in the top five African Premier Leagues and has been a top league in Africa for decades now.

According to Football Stage (n.d.), the South African football pyramid is the structure that produces, maintains, and sets the standard of football in South Africa. It is an ecosystem that, if used effectively, can help establish South African football as a leader on the continent and a prominent player globally.

THE FOOTBALL PYRAMID

There are four promotional divisions below the Premier Soccer League (PSL) in descending order:

  • 1. First Division — formerly the National First Division (NFD), now the Motsepe Foundation Championship
  • 2. Second Division — formerly Vodacom, now ABC Motsepe
  • 3. Third Division — formerly Castle, now the South African Breweries League
  • 4. Fourth Division — now called Local Football Leagues (LFA)

The Local Football Associations are managed by regional football associations spread across the nine different provinces. The Mabopane Football Association falls under the Gauteng Regional Football Association.
